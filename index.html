I am a researcher at Alibaba Group, working on the AI inference platform. My current research focuses on large-scale LLM serving systems, including GPU pooling, disaggregated architectures, request scheduling, and inference engine optimizations. I am also the maintainer of ServeGen (a framework for generating realistic LLM serving workloads), a co-creator and PPMC member of Apache GraphAr (a standardized graph storage format), and a developer of GraphScope (a one-stop large-scale graph processing system).

I received my Ph.D. degree from the MADSys Group at Tsinghua University in 2020, under the guidance of Professor Yongwei Wu.
